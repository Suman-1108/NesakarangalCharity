WELCOME TO NESAKARANGAL MINORITY EDUCATIONAL & CHARITABLE TRUST

SPROUT BLOSSOM AND YIELD

Nesakarangal Charity in Tuticorin is a registered charitable organization who works towards the upliftment of women and children in the rural areas of southern Tamil Nadu with a Motto of” SPROUT BLOSSOM AND YIELD”. Since 2004, Nesakarangal Charity in Tuticorin runs an Orphanage Nesakarangal Children Home in Tuticorin for abandoned/orphan children providing Shelter, Food, Education Skill development training along with love and care. Nesakarangal charity in Tuticorin also runs a Home for Abandoned women in Tuticorin which provides a home for old aged women who are left in the streets to survive on their own when they need help.

We also provide shelter for destitute and employment training for them to survive for a better life. Nesakarangal charity in Tuticorin fights for the rights and upliftment of women and girl children. Our organization which is now a pathway for many women and children leading towards a bright future. Our organization gives courage to many girls and women to withstand and stand up for their rights. Nesakarangal Charity in Tuticorin received Vishwa Jyoti Award in the year 2014 for best social service work .The Management has also received many Achiever’s Awards and Honorary awards for their dedicated charity work in Thoothukudi.
