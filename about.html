Our Profile

Nesakarangal is a registered charitable organization runs for the upliftment of women and children in the rural areas of southern Tamil Nadu. Since 2004 Our home is giving education to many children and giving employment to much destitute for a better life. we fights for the rights and upliftment of women. Our organization which is now a pathway for many women and children leading towards a bright future. Our organization giving an inspiration of courage to many girls to withstand and stand up for their rights. We appreciated by the Tuticorin media for our services. We received Vishwa Jyothi Award for our charitable work in 2014 and we are honored to receive achievers award for dedicated service to humanity and honored by the king's television for our dedicated social service.
