HOME FOR THE AGED WOMEN

The aim of Nesakarangal Old Age Home is to feed, to shelter, to clothe, to counsel, to medicate and to bury the abandoned destitute persons in rural areas. At present there are 40 abandoned old women and destitute are cared in the home. They are given food, clothes, medicines, counseling and shelter. They are also buried in case no relatives come forward to help. THIS Home for Aged women in tuticorin is a long-term residential healthcare family providing an atmosphere of respect and concern for each individual’s resident. The Homes provides a safe, appropriates environments specifically designed to meet the need of its aged residents who may have different geriatric problems.

SUPPORT FOR ABANDONED WOMEN

Nesakarangal is reaching out to such old aged women's and support for abandoned women's, helping them live worry-free retired lives, despite the aches of age, the pressures of poverty, and the despair of destitution in Home for Aged women in tuticorin. The NESAKARANGAL MINORITY EDUCATIONAL & CHARITABLE TRUST in Caldwell colony, Tuticorin is a warm and welcoming home. It providing for the physical, emotional and psychological needs of senior citizens who have been abandoned by their own families. Our Senior Citizen Outreach provides financial and psychosocial support to abandoned women's living a life of poverty in urban poor and remote rural regions through our outreach projects in Home for Aged women in tuticorin. The changed social environment created many problems to the senior citizens. The children and relatives of the aged are neglecting and humiliating, most of these senior citizens are poor and under privileged and face hell lot of problems for their survival. The family system and Social values have been undergoing significant change which is not favorable to the Aged.
